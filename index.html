Capabilities

Core capabilities

01

GSA Containers & Vault Doors

Inspection, repair, and replacement of GSA-approved Class 5 and 6 security containers and vault doors to FED-STD-809.

  • Preventive maintenance and inspection
  • Lockouts and emergency openings
  • Neutralization and repair after opening
  • Vault door removal and replacement
02

High-Security Locks

Installation, service, and replacement of FF-L-2740B electromechanical combination locks and related hardware.

  • Kaba Mas X-Series and CDX-10
  • Sargent & Greenleaf 2740B
  • Kaba Mas LKM10K retrofits
  • Combination changes and time locks
03

ICD-705 SCIF Doors

Installation, repair, alignment, and upgrade of the doors that keep a SCIF accredited.

  • STC-rated acoustic door assemblies
  • RF-shielded door assemblies
  • Seal, hinge, and hardware repair
04

SCIF Renovation

SCIF renovation projects already depend on the door, vault, and lock scope BSSL performs. We are building out from that scope into renovation work on sensitive compartmented facilities.

  • Self-performed: STC and RF door assemblies, vault doors, containers, and FF-L-2740B locks installed to ICD-705
  • Teaming: pursuing teaming arrangements with general contractors for walls, ceilings, and building systems, with BSSL as secure-hardware subcontractor or SDVOSB team member
  • Trained: IACET-accredited coursework in SCIF/SAPF planning, design, and construction
