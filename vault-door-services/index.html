Vault Door Repair, Installation, and Inspection

BSSL repairs, replaces, and inspects GSA Class 5 vault doors at federal and military facilities, including armories and secure storage rooms.

Photo placeholder: Ben to supply (vault door work)

Repair

Bolt work, hinges, alignment, combination and FF-L-2740B locks, and day gates.

Installation and replacement

Site survey, removal of the existing door, delivery coordination, installation of the replacement door and frame, and function checks. Examples include a GSA Class 5 armory vault door at Andersen AFB, Guam, and a vault door replacement at MCAS New River, NC. See past performance.

Inspection

Inspection and preventive maintenance by a Lockmasters GSA Certified Safe & Vault Technician & Inspector.

Credentials

  • Lockmasters GSA Certified Safe & Vault Technician & Inspector
  • Lockmasters SCIF Acoustical & RF Door Installation
  • LKM-10K Certified Installer; Certified STC and RF rated door installer
  • Singularity SCIF/SAPF Planning, Design & Construction
  • Licensed in CA, NC, and VA. Insured.

Frequently asked questions

Do you replace vault doors as well as repair them?

Yes. We handle the site survey, removal, delivery coordination, installation, and function check.

Do you install locks on vault doors?

Yes. We install and replace FF-L-2740B locks, including LKM-10K and CDX-10 locks.
